Home Home Buyer Programs Home Buyer Programs Did you know TSAHC offers down payment assistance to home buyers in both urban and rural areas? The Texas State Affordable Housing Corporation (TSAHC) provides 30-year fixed interest rate mortgage loans, down payment assistance, and mortgage credit certificates to home buyers in communities throughout Texas.  These programs are offered through a network of approved mortgage lenders. Homes for Texas Heroes Program: provides home buyer assistance specifically to teachers, police and correctional officers, fire fighters and EMS personnel, and veterans.
